## Question

What does the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (Regulation (EU) 2023/956) actually require from steel and aluminium exporters in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Oman, and Egypt — during the transitional period (Oct 2023 – Dec 2025), from 1 January 2026 (definitive regime), and at full phase-in (2034) — and where do MENA producers structurally win or lose?

## Method

- Searched: EUR-Lex full text of Regulation (EU) 2023/956 (Annex I, Annex II, Articles 1, 6, 7, 10, 15, 30, 31); Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1773 (transitional-period reporting); European Commission TAXUD CBAM pages (threshold change Omnibus I, Regulation (EU) 2025/2083); CBAM default value publications (Commission notices); industry data on MENA DRI-EAF routes (Emirates Steel Arkan, Hadeed/SABIC, Ezz Steel, Jindal Shadeed) and aluminium smelters (EGA, Alba, Ma'aden, Sohar Aluminium, Egyptalum).
- Excluded: consultancy marketing summaries, news reprints without primary anchors, and 2024-dated guidance superseded by the 2025 omnibus amendments (notably the 50-tonne de minimis threshold, which replaced the €150 consignment exemption). Any secondary figure is flagged UNVERIFIED unless the primary instrument confirms it.
- Note on article-file naming: the editorial backlog references content articles under `docs/content/`; no product/CMS code was written — this card's deliverable is this research document only.

**Correction on #20 for precision:** Annex II's structure is the reverse reading: Section A lists goods for which direct **and** indirect emissions count — including aluminium. Section B lists goods for which **only direct emissions** count — including iron & steel (HS 7218–7229, 7301–7311 groups). So the operational summary: **steel = Scope 1 only (for now); aluminium = Scope 1 + Scope 2.** Article 30's review clause may extend indirect emissions to steel later; decision owner: European Commission (review due before end of the transitional period, originally by 31 Dec 2025 report to Parliament/Council).

## Implications for the product tools

1. **CBAM embedded-emissions calculator (future TOOL-x):** the highest-value free tool Exvorta can ship for this persona. Inputs: HS heading, mass, direct-intensity (t CO2e/t), indirect factor where applicable. Must encode the Annex II steel/aluminium asymmetry (#3/#20) and warn when default values would apply. Article 7 rule (defaults only until end-2024) means post-2026 imports need actual installation data — the tool should collect "installation ID + verification status" flags.
2. **Buyer-facing document pack:** generate a CBAM data-request template (CN code, installed-production-line list, emission intensity by line, fuel mix, electricity PPA evidence) that MENA exporters can hand to EU buyers' authorised declarants. This maps 1:1 to the RFQ-cart/RFQ-pack concepts already in the content backlog (INS-11).
3. **Verifier-readiness checklist:** Accredited-verifier requirement (#8) is a hard gate from 2026. A pre-audit checklist (metering records, calorific values for gas, electricity origin proof) is a natural lead magnet for the compliance persona.
4. **Threshold triage:** with the 50-tonne mass threshold (#7), small EU buyers may fall outside CBAM — but large buyers remain fully exposed. A "does my buyer owe CBAM?" mini-check belongs on the tool hub alongside the HS-code lookup.
5. **Positioning for content:** MENA steel wins structurally on direct emissions (gas DRI-EAF vs coal BF-BOF, #13 — UNVERIFIED, needs plant data); MENA aluminium wins only if power origin is provable (EGA solar #14, Egyptalum hydro #16 — both need documentation). The article should stress **provable low-carbon power**, not mere claims.

## Open questions (decision owner noted)

- **Article 30 review outcome:** Will the Commission extend indirect-emissions accounting to iron & steel, closing the current Scope-1-only advantage? Owner: European Commission (report to Parliament & Council under Art. 30; originally due by end of 2025). This is the single biggest swing factor for MENA steel.
- **UK CBAM:** The UK has legislated its own CBAM with a planned start of 1 January 2027 — do MENA exporters need dual readiness? Owner: HM Treasury/GOV.UK legislation (final rate/dates need verification at article-writing time).
- **Electricity PPA recognition:** Which evidence of renewable PPA/GO-equivalents will CBAM verifiers accept for aluminium smelters outside the EU? Owner: Commission (implementing guidance) + accredited verifiers.
- **Emission-data confidentiality:** will non-EU producers share installation-level data with EU declarants under NDA, or use the Article 32 authorised-registration route? Owner: exporter + EU importer jointly.
- **Not verified:** the exact current CBAM factor percentages applied at each year's phase-in after any omnibus amendments; the named per-installation intensities of any specific MENA plant; UK CBAM start date and rates; Article 30 review outcome. Each requires re-checking EUR-Lex/GOV.UK at publication time and, for plant intensities, named confirmer data from the producers themselves.
